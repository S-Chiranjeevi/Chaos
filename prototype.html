Myth or fact?

“Periods should arrive on the exact same day every month.”

Not always. Period timing can vary from person to person, and sometimes from month to month.

People’s bodies and routines are different. A change can sometimes be related to stress, illness, or other things. This short article cannot tell you the reason for a change in your body.

What can you do?

If it helps, write down the dates you notice. You can bring your notes and questions to a health worker, who can talk with you about your situation.
